    01 / About

    Where mechanical design meets intelligent software

    Vaishak VMechatronics Engineer

    I build at the intersection of mechanical engineering, robotics, and software.

    I work across both sides of robotics: the mechanical design that gives a robot its structure and motion, and the software that lets it perceive, plan, and act. That means CAD and mechanism design on one side, and ROS2, computer vision, and navigation on the other.

    I enjoy the problems where the two have to work together, where a mechanism's constraints shape the software, and what the software needs shapes the hardware.
